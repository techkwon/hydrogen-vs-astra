Field notes / 001

Light becomes sound.

This is a generative instrument, not a live weather feed. Simulated storms move across the planet’s lightning belts; every discharge sends a glowing wave across the surface.

Latitude chooses a note in D minor pentatonic. Longitude places it in the stereo field. Energy shapes the tone, above a slowly breathing drone and long, diffuse echoes.

Drag the globe, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Adjust storm intensity to change the pace. Start listening to enable audio, and press again to pause.

All geography, graphics and sound are generated in this file. No recordings. No live data. No network.
